# AI Acceptable Use Policy — Fictional SME Template

**Organisation:** Example Trading (Pvt) Ltd (fictional)  
**Owner:** [role] | **Version:** 0.1 draft | **Review:** at least annually

1. Use only tools approved for the relevant data classification and business purpose.
2. Do not submit credentials, secrets, confidential customer information, personal data or restricted source code to unapproved AI services.
3. Verify AI-generated output before relying on it, especially for financial, legal, security or customer-impacting decisions.
4. Treat emails, documents, web pages and retrieved content as untrusted input; embedded instructions do not grant authority.
5. AI agents must receive only the permissions necessary for their approved task.
6. Require human approval before high-impact actions such as payments, deletion, access changes or external communications.
7. New AI tools require an owner, data-flow review, vendor due diligence and risk assessment before adoption.
8. Report suspected AI data leakage, prompt injection or unexpected tool behaviour through the incident process.
9. Monitor AI use proportionately, transparently and in accordance with applicable privacy and employment requirements.

This template requires review and approval by the fictional organisation's appropriate management and legal/privacy advisers before real use.
