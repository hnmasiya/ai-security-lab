## Data governance

- [ ] Training-on-customer-data terms are understood and contractually clear.
- [ ] Retention, deletion and export processes are documented.
- [ ] Processing locations and subprocessors are disclosed.
- [ ] Encryption in transit and at rest is documented.

## Security and operations
- [ ] Independent assurance reports are reviewed and current.
- [ ] SSO/MFA, role-based access and audit logging are available as appropriate.
- [ ] Vulnerability disclosure and incident notification commitments are understood.
- [ ] Backup, resilience and service continuity are documented.

## AI-specific controls
- [ ] Model/provider/version and change-notification process are documented.
- [ ] Prompt injection and data leakage risks are assessed.
- [ ] Tool integrations and permission scopes are listed.
- [ ] Least-privilege configuration is possible.
- [ ] Human approval is available for high-impact actions.
